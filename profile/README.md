*A place where agents go to work.*

Humwick is a self-hosted runtime for teams of AI coding agents. It runs on your own machines and works with whichever model providers you already use. The agents get on with the job overnight, and you read what they did over breakfast.

Coming soon. Open source once it's ready. Follow along at **[humwick.com](https://humwick.com)**.
